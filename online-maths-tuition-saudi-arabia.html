CBSE and ICSE maths for students in Saudi Arabia

Indian schools in Saudi Arabia mostly follow CBSE. If your child is on ICSE, or will move to an ICSE school in Kerala later, those classes are available too.

CBSE

CBSE schools in Saudi Arabia use the same NCERT textbooks and sit the same board exams as schools in India, so classes follow the same chapters and exam pattern.

  • Classes 5 to 8 foundations
  • Class 9 and 10 board preparation
  • Class 11 and 12 maths
  • NCERT exercises and exemplar practice
  • Previous years' board papers
ICSE and ISC

The ICSE syllabus has topics CBSE does not, and expects fuller written working. Useful too for families planning a move to an ICSE school in Kerala.

  • GST, Banking, Shares and Dividends
  • Matrices
  • Loci and Similarity
  • Step-by-step working for full marks
  • ISC maths for Classes 11 and 12
