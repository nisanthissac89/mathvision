What is taught

Mathematics only, from Class 5 to Class 12, following the board your child's school follows. Progress is checked with regular worksheets and exams.

CBSE 9 and 10

NCERT chapters covered in step with school, with board-style practice in Classes 9 and 10. Class 10 includes:

  • Real Numbers
  • Polynomials
  • Linear Equations in Two Variables
  • Quadratic Equations
  • Arithmetic Progressions
  • Triangles
  • Coordinate Geometry
  • Trigonometry
  • Circles
  • Surface Areas and Volumes
  • Statistics
  • Probability
ICSE 9 and 10

The ICSE syllabus asks for more written working and has topics CBSE does not. Class 10 includes:

  • GST
  • Banking
  • Shares and Dividends
  • Linear Inequations
  • Quadratic Equations
  • Ratio and Proportion
  • Matrices
  • Similarity
  • Loci
  • Circles
  • Mensuration
  • Trigonometry
  • Statistics and Probability
Classes 11 and 12

Higher secondary maths for CBSE and ISC students, where the pace and the depth both jump. Topics include:

  • Sets, Relations and Functions
  • Trigonometric Functions
  • Complex Numbers
  • Permutations and Combinations
  • Sequences and Series
  • Conic Sections
  • Limits and Derivatives
  • Matrices and Determinants
  • Differentiation
  • Integrals
  • Differential Equations
  • Vectors and 3D Geometry
  • Probability
Classes 5 to 8

Middle school is where gaps start. These classes build the basics that later chapters depend on:

  • Fractions and Decimals
  • Factors and Multiples
  • Integers
  • Ratio and Percentage
  • Basic Algebra
  • Geometry
  • Word Problems

Questions parents ask

Which classes and boards do you teach?

Mathematics for CBSE and ICSE students from Class 5 to Class 12, including ISC for Classes 11 and 12.

Is there a trial class?

Yes. The first class is a free trial, so your child can see how the teaching feels before you decide.

We live in the UAE or Saudi Arabia. Can my child join?

Yes. Current students join online from Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Saudi Arabia. Class times are fixed to suit Gulf time.

How do online classes work?

Classes are live on Google Meet. Nidhi works through each problem on a writing tablet, so your child sees every step written out as on a classroom board.

Is it one-to-one or group tuition?

Both are available. You can choose one-to-one classes or a small group.

How will I know how my child is doing?

Through regular worksheets and exams, which show what has been understood and what needs another look.

Which language are classes taught in?

Classes are taught in English, the same language as the school textbook and the exam.

What are the fees and timings?

Fees depend on the class and on whether you choose one-to-one or group tuition. Timings are arranged to suit you. Message on WhatsApp for the current details.

My child is weak in the basics. Is it too late?

No. Most difficulty in Classes 8 to 10 comes from gaps in earlier topics such as fractions, integers and basic algebra. Those are revisited as the syllabus needs them.
